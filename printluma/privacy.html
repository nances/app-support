Documents and permissions

Imported documents, scans, photos and generated files are stored in the app’s local storage. Scanning, image processing and text recognition run on your device. Printluma does not upload your document contents to its servers. Your Apple device backups may include these files according to your backup settings. Camera access is used for scanning and capture. System pickers let you choose individual photos, contacts and files. Device authentication protects the app when you enable App lock; we never receive biometric data.

Printing, websites and sharing

iOS sends printable content to the printer you select. Sharing passes selected files to the destination you choose. The built-in browser requests the websites you enter; those websites may collect network information or use cookies under their own policies.

Subscriptions and Adapty

Apple processes payment. Adapty processes purchase and subscription records, a pseudonymous profile identifier, app/device information and SDK diagnostic information to validate entitlements and operate subscription analytics. We do not receive payment card details. Advertising identifier and IP collection are disabled in the client SDK configuration. See Adapty’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Retention and your choices

Manage or delete files inside the app. Recently deleted files can be restored for up to 30 days and can be permanently removed earlier. Uninstalling removes local app storage but does not cancel a subscription or erase records that Apple or Adapty must retain. Contact us for access or deletion requests concerning subscription-related data, and we will process requests subject to applicable retention requirements. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Children and changes

This app is a general-purpose document utility and is not directed to children. We may update this notice when functionality or processing changes and will revise its effective date.
