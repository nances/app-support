Scan, prepare and print your everyday documents and photos.

Find your printer

Connect your iPhone or iPad and AirPrint-enabled printer to the same Wi-Fi network. Wake the printer and check paper and ink. Guest networks can prevent device discovery. Printluma does not provide universal Bluetooth or USB printer drivers.

Print a document

Import a PDF or photo, review it, and choose Print document. Select your printer and available settings in the system printing panel. For Office files with complex layouts, export a PDF from the original app first.

Subscriptions

Try three print jobs free. Pro unlocks unlimited printing. Editing, saving and sharing remain available. Prices and billing periods are displayed before purchase. Restore purchases in Settings using the same Apple Account. Manage or cancel subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
