Free and Pro features

Read, import, organize, and share unchanged PDFs for free. You can try 3 successful advanced outputs. Failed actions, previews, and cancellations before an output is created do not spend a use. Pro unlocks unlimited advanced local outputs.

Available subscriptions and any eligible introductory trial are shown in the app before purchase. Apple determines your local price and trial eligibility. Original-content text editing, Office conversion, AI, and cloud collaboration are not included.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in PDF Editor, choose Get Premium, and tap Restore. Use the Apple Account that purchased the subscription.

Cancel or request a refund

Manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. For refund requests, visit Apple's refund service.

Keep a backup

Documents are stored in the app on your device. Share or export important files before deleting the app. Password-protected PDFs cannot be recovered without the password you set.

Scanning and compression

Scanning needs a supported device and camera permission. OCR accuracy depends on the source image. Compression creates an image-based copy and can remove selectable text, links, interactive forms, and editable annotations. Your original is kept, and no compressed copy is saved if the result would be larger.
