Your subscription

你的订阅

Where can I see plans and prices?在哪里查看方案与价格？

Open the Premium entry on the main page. Available plans and localized prices come from the App Store. Review the period and total amount shown by Apple before confirming. Core translation features require an active subscription.

在一级页面打开会员入口。可购买套餐和本地化价格由 App Store 返回，请在确认前查看 Apple 显示的订阅周期与总金额。核心翻译功能需要有效订阅。

I subscribed, but access is not available.已经订阅，为什么没有解锁？

Check that the subscription is active under your Apple Account and that you are using the account that made the purchase. Connect to the internet, then choose Restore purchases in Sayvori. A pending purchase may need approval. An expired subscription does not restore active access.

先在 Apple 账户中确认订阅有效，并使用购买时的账户。联网后在 Sayvori 选择“恢复购买”。待批准的购买可能需要先完成批准；已到期的订阅不会恢复有效权益。

How do I cancel or request a refund?如何取消订阅或申请退款？

Use Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your iPhone, or Manage subscription inside Sayvori. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple manages billing and refund decisions. Visit Apple’s purchase support for refund requests.

在 iPhone“设置 → Apple 账户 → 订阅”中管理，或在 Sayvori 内选择“管理订阅”。删除 App 不会取消订阅。Apple 负责计费与退款决定；退款申请请使用 Apple 购买支持。

Prices are temporarily unavailable.暂时无法加载订阅价格。

Check your connection and App Store availability, then retry. Do not confirm a purchase unless the App Store shows the intended product, period and total price. Restoring purchases will not start a new subscription.

检查网络与 App Store 是否可用后重试。在 Apple 显示正确产品、周期与总价前不要确认购买。“恢复购买”不会新建一笔订阅。

Report an inaccurate or inappropriate AI answer反馈错误或不当的 AI 回答

Email support with the issue, selected language and approximate time. Include an excerpt only if you choose to and remove personal information. We review reports and address confirmed issues. AI output may be inaccurate or inappropriate: do not rely on it for medical, legal, financial or emergency decisions.

请通过客服邮箱提供问题、所选语言和大致发生时间。是否附上内容片段由你决定，请先删除个人信息。我们会检查反馈并处理确认的问题。AI 输出可能错误或不恰当，不应作为医疗、法律、财务或紧急事项的决策依据。

Translation & photo help

翻译与拍照帮助

The online translation is taking longer than expected.在线翻译等待时间较长。

Keep the app open while a request is processing. You can cancel and retry without re-entering your text. A backup provider may be used when the primary service cannot complete a request. For long text, try smaller sections. AI output can contain mistakes; verify critical wording.

请求进行中请保持 App 打开；可以取消后重试，无需重新输入文字。主服务无法完成请求时，可能切换备用服务。长文本可分段尝试。AI 输出可能出错，重要内容请核对。

Photo recognition missed part of the text.拍照没有识别出完整文字。

Use a sharp, well-lit image with readable text. Keep the page flat and reduce reflections. Recognition runs on the device; online translation receives the recognized text. Review names, numbers and handwritten text carefully.

使用清晰、光照均匀且文字可辨的照片，尽量保持页面平整并减少反光。识别在设备上进行，在线翻译接收识别出的文字。姓名、数字和手写内容尤其需要核对。

Voice input is unavailable.语音输入不可用。

Allow microphone and speech recognition access in iOS Settings. Availability depends on the language, device and Apple’s speech service. Some speech recognition may require a network connection. You can still type text instead.

请在 iOS 设置中允许麦克风和语音识别权限。可用性取决于语言、设备及 Apple 语音服务，部分语音识别需要联网；也可以改为输入文字。

How does offline translation work?离线翻译如何使用？

Choose supported on-device languages and download Apple’s required language models while online. Language support and downloads depend on the OS and device. Online AI assistance and online provider translation still need a connection; downloading a language does not make those services offline.

联网时选择设备支持的语言，并下载 Apple 所需的语言模型。支持情况取决于系统与设备。在线 AI 助手和供应商在线翻译仍需网络，下载语言不会让这些在线服务变成离线服务。
