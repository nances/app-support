Core translation features require Premium. Weekly (one week), monthly (one month) and yearly (one year) auto-renewable plans provide the same Premium access for their respective periods. Use Restore purchases with the purchasing Apple Account to recover eligible access. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, subject to applicable rights and Apple’s decision.
