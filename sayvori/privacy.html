Yaqian Li operates AI Translate · Sayvori. This notice describes the iOS app, this support website, and the services used to provide them. It separates on-device processing from online processing. Features and service providers may vary by device, language and service availability.

1. What the app processes

InformationPurpose and destination
Text you submit for online translationSent to the configured translation service so it can produce a translation. The route may use OpenRouter and its model providers, or independent DeepSeek as a backup.

Camera or selected photo contentRecognition runs on the device. The current online translation path sends recognized text or detected object names, rather than the original image, to AI services.

These support purchase validation, restoration, revenue analytics and subscription presentation. We do not receive your full payment-card details.

2. Online providers

Online features transmit the content you submit to the selected service. A request may be sent to a backup provider if the primary route fails. Provider policies and routing settings govern their handling, retention and other processing. We do not promise zero retention or that every model provider excludes training use.

  • OpenRouter privacy policy and model provider information
  • DeepSeek privacy policy
  • Adapty end-user privacy policy
  • Apple speech recognition and dictation information

OpenRouter routes requests to third-party model hosts; the host may vary with availability. It receives the same submitted content needed to run the chosen model. OpenRouter and model hosts may process data outside your country; DeepSeek states that its services store collected information in the People’s Republic of China. Applicable protections, retention and legal rights can differ by location. Provider retention may include security, abuse prevention, legal compliance or service improvement; do not assume that a submitted prompt is deleted immediately after an answer. We do not configure a universal zero-retention or no-training guarantee for these routes. Only submit content you are permitted to share with these services.

3. Your controls

Before the first online AI request, a separate prompt identifies the recipients and content and asks you to allow or decline. Declining prevents that request; you may still browse the app, manage local records and use available on-device features, subject to their subscription requirements. You can withdraw permission for online AI processing in Settings → Privacy & data. This prevents new online requests; it does not recall requests already sent or erase copies already held by providers. An in-progress request may still finish; cancel the request to stop waiting for its result. You can delete individual translation records and clear the AI conversation in the app. Camera, microphone and speech-recognition permissions can be changed in iOS Settings. Supported on-device translation uses Apple’s downloaded language models; online AI features still require a network connection.

Manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account or the app’s Manage subscription control. Removing the app does not cancel an Apple subscription. Deleting local records does not automatically delete records that Apple, Adapty or an AI provider must retain under their own policies.

4. Identifiers, diagnostics and retention

The current Adapty initialization disables IDFA collection and SDK IP-address collection. Network services can still receive connection metadata necessary to deliver requests. Subscription records may be retained for verification, billing, fraud prevention and legal obligations according to the relevant service’s policy.

Local records remain until removed through the app or through applicable device-storage controls. The app connects directly to the named AI services; we do not operate a translation proxy or store your translation text on our own server. Those services process requests under their policies. We do not set their retention periods.

5. This support website

This static support site stores only your language choice in browser local storage in its own application code. It does not contain a contact form, advertising tracker or translation submission feature. Its hosting platform may process access and security information; access to the published support pages does not require an app account.

Contact Yaqian Li at 739863741@qq.com for support, content concerns or privacy requests. Your email address and the information you choose to include are processed to investigate and respond. Send only the minimum needed; do not send passwords, verification codes or full payment details. We may need proportionate information to verify and locate a request. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. Where relevant records are controlled by Apple or a service provider, we will explain the appropriate route; local deletion cannot erase records held by those parties. 7. Children and policy updates

Sayvori is a general-purpose translation app, not a service directed at children. Do not submit personal information about children or other people without appropriate permission. If you believe a child’s data was shared improperly, contact us. Material changes to our recipients or purposes will be explained before the affected processing; this page shows the effective update date.
